01

Step One

Discovery & Brief

Every great design project starts with deep understanding. We begin with a comprehensive discovery session — either in person at our Chennai studio or via video call — to understand your business, goals, audience, and competitive landscape.

We ask the right questions so we're solving the right problems. You'll fill in our detailed project brief, which becomes our shared roadmap for the entire project.

⏱ 1–2 days

What happens in this phase:

  • Initial consultation call (30–60 minutes)
  • Detailed project brief document shared
  • Competitor analysis & market research
  • Target audience profiling
  • Project scope, timeline & pricing confirmed
  • 50% deposit invoice issued
02

Step Two

Strategy & Concept

With a clear brief in hand, our team develops the creative strategy. We research your industry, analyze successful competitors, and build a visual direction — mood boards, color exploration, and typographic concepts — that will guide the design work.

You'll see and approve the creative direction before we commit to execution, ensuring we're perfectly aligned on the aesthetic and strategic vision.

⏱ 2–3 days

What happens in this phase:

  • Industry & trend research
  • Mood board creation (2–3 directions)
  • Color palette exploration
  • Typography pairing selections
  • Creative direction presentation
  • Client sign-off before design begins
03

Step Three

Design & Refinement

This is where the magic happens. Our designers craft the initial concepts using the approved creative direction. We present multiple design directions for your feedback and iterate until every detail is perfect.

All feedback is handled through a structured revision process — you receive designs via a shared link, leave comments, and we implement changes. Clear, efficient, and never frustrating.

⏱ 7–14 days

What happens in this phase:

  • Initial concept designs presented (2–3 options)
  • Feedback collected via shared Figma or PDF
  • Revisions implemented (2–unlimited rounds)
  • Design system built & documented
  • All formats prepared for final use
  • Final approval from client
04

Step Four

Delivery & Handoff

Once designs are approved, we prepare a comprehensive, well-organised file package. Everything you need is included — source files, export-ready formats, and full documentation so you can use your new brand confidently across any medium.

We don't just hand off files and disappear. We offer a 30-day post-project support period to answer any questions that arise after launch.

⏱ 1–2 days

What's in your delivery package:

  • Source files (AI, PSD, Figma)
  • Export-ready files (PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF)
  • Brand guidelines document
  • Font licenses & usage instructions
  • Color codes (HEX, CMYK, RGB, Pantone)
  • 30-day post-delivery support included

FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project with us.

How long does a typical project take?

Most brand identity projects take 2–3 weeks. Larger projects like full brand systems with website design can take 4–8 weeks. We'll give you a precise timeline in your project brief.

How many revisions are included?

Our Professional and Enterprise packages include unlimited revisions. The Starter package includes 2 rounds of revisions, with additional rounds available at ₹2,000 per round.

Do I own the designs after delivery?

Yes, absolutely. Once the final payment is received, full ownership and all intellectual property rights transfer to you. The source files are yours to use however you need.

What formats will I receive?

You'll receive files in every format you need: AI, EPS, PSD for editing; PNG and JPG for digital use; PDF and SVG for web and print — all clearly organised in a folder structure.

Can we meet in person?

Yes! Our studio is located in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. We welcome in-person meetings for discovery sessions and design presentations. Remote clients are equally welcome via Zoom.

What is your payment structure?

We require 50% upfront to begin the project and the remaining 50% upon final delivery. For enterprise projects, a custom payment schedule can be arranged.

Ready to get started?

Tell us about your project. We'll respond within 24 hours with a proposal.
