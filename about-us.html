Our Story

Built from a passion for purposeful design

PixelCraft Studio was founded in 2017 by Karthik Selvam, a graphic designer with a vision to bring world-class design to businesses in Tamil Nadu and beyond. What started as a one-person freelance studio in a small office in Nungambakkam has grown into a full-service creative agency with a team of 10 specialists.

Over 8 years, we've completed more than 120 projects for clients ranging from local cafés and startups to established enterprises. Every project, big or small, receives the same level of care, craft, and strategic thinking.

We believe that great design isn't just about aesthetics — it's a business tool that builds trust, attracts customers, and creates lasting impressions. That belief drives everything we create.

What We Believe

Our values guide everything

These principles shape how we work, how we treat our clients, and the quality of work we deliver.

◈

Craft Over Everything

We take genuine pride in every pixel. No shortcuts, no templates, no lazy design. Every project receives meticulous attention to detail and a genuine creative investment from our team.

◎

Strategy Before Aesthetics

Beautiful design that doesn't serve a purpose is just decoration. We always start with understanding your business goals, then design solutions that are both stunning and strategically sound.

✦

Transparency & Trust

No surprises. We communicate clearly about timelines, pricing, and creative decisions. We believe the best client relationships are built on honesty and mutual respect.

▤

Collaboration Over Dictation

You know your business better than anyone. We're here to bring your vision to life, not impose our own. The best results come from genuine creative partnership.

⬡

Continuous Growth

Design trends evolve constantly. Our team invests in learning, attending workshops, and experimenting with new tools to ensure our work always reflects the best of contemporary design.

▣

Long-Term Partnerships

We're not interested in one-off transactions. We aim to become your trusted design partner for the long haul — growing with your brand as your business evolves.
